# Team 2 Starter Task - Metrics and Calculations

## Team mission
Turn cleaned data into meaningful numerical indicators for decision-making.

## Student starter tasks
1. Open `script.js` and review `calculateMetrics()`.
2. Add at least two new metrics, for example:
   - `activePercentage`
   - `inactivePercentage`
   - `debrisPerSatellite`
3. Ensure calculations are accurate when filters are changed.
4. Update `renderKpis()` to display your added metrics.
5. Format percentages and large numbers for readability (for example `toFixed(1)` and `toLocaleString("en-GB")`).
6. Add comments explaining each formula.

## Success criteria (how you know you have succeeded)
- KPI values update correctly when filter controls change.
- No divide-by-zero errors occur.
- All formulas are understandable to another team member.
- Your metrics help Team 4 write stronger recommendations.

## Suggested stretch task
Add a trend score that compares recent debris totals with earlier values.
