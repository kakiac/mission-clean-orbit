# Team 5 Starter Task - Agile Check-ins, Integration and QA

## Team mission
Act as agile facilitators and quality coordinators across all teams.

## Key role note
Team 5 will do less direct coding than other teams. Your primary responsibility is to run structured agile check-ins and ensure integration quality.

## Student starter tasks
1. Create a mini sprint board (paper, whiteboard or markdown) with columns:
   - To Do
   - In Progress
   - Blocked
   - Done
2. Run check-ins every 5-7 minutes with each team. Ask:
   - What did you complete?
   - What are you doing next?
   - What is blocked?
3. Record blockers and assign escalation actions.
4. During integration, verify each team has met success criteria.
5. Update the QA panel in the dashboard with:
   - integration status,
   - key risks,
   - go/no-go recommendation for presentation.
6. Lead final smoke test:
   - page loads,
   - filters work,
   - KPIs render,
   - insights update,
   - no critical console errors.

## Success criteria (how you know you have succeeded)
- Every team received regular structured check-ins.
- Blockers were logged and followed up.
- Integration completed with clear test outcomes.
- Final go/no-go recommendation was justified with evidence.
- Team communication improved because of your facilitation.

## Suggested stretch task
Create a "definition of done" checklist and require team sign-off before final demo.
